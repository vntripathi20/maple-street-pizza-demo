How it's made

The 48-hour clock

No pre-made dough, no par-baked shells. Here is what happens between the flour bag and your box.

  1. Day 1 · 6:00amMix

    Flour, water, salt, a little yeast.

    Dough is mixed in small batches each morning so the whole day's supply is always the same age.

  2. Day 1 · 10:00amRest

    Portioned, balled, and put in the cold.

    Every ball sits in the walk-in for 48 hours. That slow ferment is where the flavor and the airy, chewy crumb come from.

  3. Day 3 · 11:00amStretch

    Out of the cold, into our hands.

    Balls warm up on the bench. When your order comes in, one is stretched by hand. Never rolled, never pressed.

  4. Your order · 90 secFire

    Sauce, cheese, toppings. Then the oven.

    The oven runs around 900°F. Your pizza comes out when the crust is blistered and the cheese is bubbling, usually a minute and a half later.

When the day's dough runs out, we're out of pizza. On Fridays, call ahead.
