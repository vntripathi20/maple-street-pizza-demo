Our story

Made for sharing, since day one.

Maple Street Pizza started with one oven, one family recipe and a simple idea: a table of friends, a hot pie in the middle, and nowhere else to be.

A decade later, nothing has changed except the line out the door. We still make the dough by hand, we still know most of our regulars by name, and we still believe the best meals are the ones you pass around.

  • Dough made fresh every morning
  • Local produce when it's in season
  • Dine-in, takeout and easy call-ahead pickup

How we make it

From dough to doorstep in four steps

No shortcuts. That's the whole secret.

Ferment

Dough rests for 48 hours to build flavor and a light, chewy texture.

Stretch

Every pie is stretched by hand. Never rolled, never rushed.

Top

Sweet tomato, fresh cheese and toppings added to order.

Fire

Into the wood-fired oven and out in minutes, blistered and bubbling.
